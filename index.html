    Cuts

      Press X or Cut here to drop a second at the playhead, then drag the red bar's ends. Playback and exports skip cut parts.

      Speed-ups

        Press S or Speed up to fast-forward 2 s at the playhead (2×, 4× or 8×), then drag the teal bar's ends. Sped-up parts are muted.

        Text

          Press T or Add text to put a caption on screen for 3 s. Drag it on the preview to move it, press A or Arrow to point at something, and drag the violet bar's ends to change when it shows.

          Blur

            Hide emails, names or keys for the whole clip or part of it, or pick Spotlight to dim everything around a box. Add a box, drag it over the spot on the preview and drag its corner to resize it.

            Zooms

              Move the playhead to a moment worth a closer look, then press Add zoom here.

              Drag the ends of an orange bar on the timeline to make a zoom longer or shorter.

              Finds moments where only part of the screen changes and zooms in on them.
